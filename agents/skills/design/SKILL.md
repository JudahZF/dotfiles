# Design

## Core principles

- Avoid designs that felt templated.
- Ensure designs have a unified aesthetic with consistent palette, typography, and layout.

Animations greatly improve the experience of the site, **but only if done well**. Add various animations on hover, scroll or just ambiently where appropriate, but don't overdo it. Continuos animations are computationally expensive and distracting, use them sparingly or just don't use them.

## Homepages

For web designs, build a design around the subject, with a hero as the thesis. Make it a characteristic representation, in whatever form makes sense. The structure of the site can then be used to convey meaning and information to the audience, and structural devices should be used to say something about the content, not just look pretty.

## Dashboards and Apps

Conversely, dashboards should be built around user experience and data. Avoid large paddings and dense clutter, instead aim to make the application easy to use, good looking and informative.
